This house was made with one intention: rest. Rest for your body, and rest
for your mind. It stands in Almoçageme, a small village inside the Sintra-Cascais
Natural Park on the coast west of Lisbon, about 40 minutes from the city by car
and also reachable by public transport. The Sintra forest rises on one side and
the Atlantic Ocean lies on the other. Praia Grande and Praia da Adraga are
within walking distance, and the forest trails are a walk away from the house.

Here you can pair a good night's sleep with a nature walk in the morning, an
hour by the ocean in the afternoon and an early evening, and out of those
ordinary days you build your own bespoke retreat.

The house, the location, the inside and the outside all make it possible to be
in alignment with your circadian rhythm. Light sets the day: you wake with the
sun, spend the hours walking in the forest or by the ocean, and go to sleep
when the sky turns dark. Eating local fruit and vegetables is easy too, there
is a small shop with local produce around the corner from the house.

Inside, natural materials dominate: wood, stone, plaster, terracotta and
linen. Being surrounded by nature outside and by natural materials inside makes the
stay one continuous, integral experience, with the same textures running from
the forest path to the bedroom.

The furniture objects were curated one by one, and most of them are second
hand, found and restored rather than bought new. This is how we embody one of
our values: an acknowledgement of our footprint. A restored chair or table
carries its previous life with it and asks very little new of the planet. It
also gives the house a calm that new things rarely have.

If you are looking for a place to stay near Sintra and the beaches of
the Sintra coast, we have two private rooms, each for up to two guests, with
shared spaces: a living room with a fireplace, an open kitchen and a terrace
with distant views of the Atlantic. You can check dates and book a room right
here.
