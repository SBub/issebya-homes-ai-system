Almoçageme is a village inside the Sintra-Cascais Natural Park, about fifteen
minutes on foot from Praia Grande and a short drive from Colares. It is quiet in
a way the coast road is not. Most of what there is to do here is outside.

## The beach, first

Praia Grande is the obvious one: a long flat stretch with a reliable beach
break, lifeguards through the summer, and enough room that it never feels full
before noon. If the swell is up, walk the cliff path north to Praia da Adraga
instead, where the water is colder and the cove is smaller.

## Then the bread

There is a bakery in the village that opens early and sells out by mid-morning.
Go before ten. The coffee is fifty cents and you drink it standing up, which is
the correct way.

## Where to sleep

We have two private rooms with shared spaces (a kitchen, a living room with a
fireplace, and a terrace that looks out toward the Atlantic). Both rooms take a
maximum of two people.

## Getting here without a car

The 1253 bus runs from Sintra station to Almoçageme and takes about thirty
minutes. From Lisbon, take the train to Sintra first. It is slower than driving
and considerably less stressful than parking near the beach in August.

## The walk back up the hill

Whatever you do during the day, you finish it by walking back up. It is steeper
than it looks on the way down. This is the part everyone remembers.
