The kitchen is on the upper floor, open to the second living area, with the
terrace just beyond it. It is shared by whoever is staying, which in practice
means at most four people.

## What is already there

Everything you need to cook a real meal: a full-size oven, a four-burner hob,
pots, pans, knives that are actually sharp, a kettle, a coffee grinder, and a
moka pot. Olive oil, salt, pepper and a few basics live in the cupboard by the
window and are there for everyone.

## The two rules

1. Wash up before you leave the room, not before you leave the house.
2. Label anything in the fridge you want to keep. Anything unlabelled on the day
   you check out gets cleared.

That is all of them. There is no schedule and no rota.

## Eating outside instead

The terrace has a table that seats four and gets the last of the sun. Most
guests end up eating out there even when they had not planned to, which is why
the kitchen tends to stay clean without much effort.
