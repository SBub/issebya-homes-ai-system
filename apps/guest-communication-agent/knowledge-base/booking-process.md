# Booking Process

## How to book a room

1. Visit www.issebya.com and choose Room 1 or Room 2
2. Select check-in and check-out dates on the calendar
3. Blocked dates are shown as unavailable
4. Enter number of guests (1 or 2) and email address
5. Click "book" to proceed to Stripe Checkout
6. Pay securely with credit card
7. Receive email confirmation with booking details

## After booking

- Confirmation email sent immediately with booking summary

## Availability

- Calendar updates in real time
- If dates show as unavailable, they are already booked
