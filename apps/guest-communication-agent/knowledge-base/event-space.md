# Event Space

The house is available for intimate gatherings. A place to cook, share a meal, or spend time together — held in a quiet, layered space by the Atlantic.

## Spaces

- Dining table for 8 in the open kitchen
- Terrace seating for 6, with distant ocean views — perfect for slow lunches
- Ground floor: front garden with a firepit
- Living room with a fireplace — cozy spot to gather between courses
- Dining area and bar setup flows between kitchen, terrace, living room, and garden

## Capacity

- Recommended: 8 guests

## Pricing

- €50 per hour
- Minimum booking of 4 hours

## Suitable for

- Dinners and lunches
- Birthdays
- Creative meetings
- Quiet celebrations
