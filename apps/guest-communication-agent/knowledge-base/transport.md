# Transport

## Distance from Lisbon

- Around 29 km from Lisbon Airport / central Lisbon
- No car needed, but one makes getting around easier

## Airport transfer

- Uber/Bolt: around €24-25 from Lisbon Airport, roughly 30-40 minutes
- Train + bus: train from Lisbon to Sintra, then bus 1241 or 1624 from Sintra to Almoçageme
