# Waste and Recycling

## Waste and recycling

In the kitchen, separate bins for:
- Paper
- Plastic & metal
- Glass
- General waste

If bins are full:
- General waste → large public bin on the main road (https://maps.app.goo.gl/YYUzkApipx6iww3N6)
- Recycling → marked containers nearby (https://maps.app.goo.gl/fm5ZMzk1YHz68hdBA)
