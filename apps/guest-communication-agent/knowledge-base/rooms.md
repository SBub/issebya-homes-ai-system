# Rooms

## Room 1 — Private Room with Shared Spaces

- Located on the ground floor of a three-level house
- Opens onto the front garden
- Has access to its own bathroom (not en suite)
- Room includes a desk
- Maximum capacity: 2 persons
- On the same level: shared living room with a fireplace and a desk
- Upstairs: open kitchen and a second living area
- Kitchen is fully equipped
- Terrace beyond the kitchen offers distant views of the Atlantic
- All spaces except the guest bedroom and bathroom are shared
- Bed: 160 × 200 cm (queen)

## Room 2 — Private Room with Shared Spaces

- Located on the ground floor of a three-level house
- Has its own en suite bathroom
- Room includes a desk
- Maximum capacity: 2 persons
- On the same level: shared living room with a fireplace and a desk
- Upstairs: open kitchen and a second living area
- Kitchen is fully equipped
- Terrace beyond the kitchen offers distant views of the Atlantic
- All spaces except the guest bedroom and bathroom are shared
- Bed: 140 × 190 cm (double)

## Key differences between rooms

- Room 1: bathroom is separate (not en suite), room opens onto the front garden, larger bed (160 × 200 cm)
- Room 2: bathroom is en suite, slightly smaller bed (140 × 190 cm)
- Both rooms are on the ground floor, same price, same capacity
